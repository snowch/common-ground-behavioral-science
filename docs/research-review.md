# Research basis and scope

The course uses established terminology, peer-reviewed theory and experiments, reviews and professional evaluation guidance. Fictional cases and illustrative rates are original teaching examples, not study results. Core caveats remain in the learning path; optional research disclosures provide citations.

DOI identities were checked against Crossref on 21 November 2026. The COM-B framework definitions and stated limits were checked in open full text. Accessible J-PAL and Magenta Book guidance was consulted; publisher abstracts support bounded descriptions of the cited empirical work. OECD metadata was verified; its website restricted automated retrieval, so no full-text review is claimed. The initial OECD DOI ending e was corrected to the verified ending f. Nudging reviews with differing conclusions are included together. This is a selective internal check, not a systematic review or independent academic review.
